What it does

Verified psychology
meets your feed.

HopeScroll wraps two of the most evidence-backed psychological tools — journaling and intention setting — around the moment you actually need them: before and after you scroll.

Sentiment analysis reads the emotional texture of every video and filters content that conflicts with your stated goals. You decide what you consume — not the algorithm.

Over time, you build a personal map of how different content affects your mood, motivation, and attention.

User flow

Six moments that change
how you scroll.

1

Set up your feeds

Download HopeScroll and connect the social feeds you want help with. The app works alongside your existing apps — no need to change platforms.

Onboarding
2

Set your intention before you open a feed

Each session begins with three simple questions: how long do you want to scroll, what do you hope to feel, and what is your current motivation level?

Intention Setting
3

Scroll with live sentiment filtering

As you browse, HopeScroll analyzes video captions and comments in real time — filtering content that conflicts with the emotional state you set out to cultivate.

Sentiment Analysis · YouTube Data API
4

End-of-session journal prompt

When your intended time is up, HopeScroll surfaces a brief reflective journal screen. How do you feel now? Did the session serve you?

Journaling
5

Your emotion matrix

Your post-session state is plotted on an emotion matrix and compared against an analysis of everything you consumed. You see the connection in real terms.

Emotion Mapping · Anthropic API
6

Long-term trends & digital health planning

Outside of sessions, explore emotional trends across time. Optionally build a personal digital health plan that aligns with your goals as a student and new adult.

Insights Dashboard
